# π₀.₅ real-robot evaluation (40 trials, 2026-11-26)

**English** · [简体中文](pi05.zh-CN.md)

- Model: [`Yufeng1216/pi05_so101_pick_place`](https://huggingface.co/Yufeng1216/pi05_so101_pick_place) (full fine-tune of `lerobot/pi05_base`, 3k steps × batch 32, float32)
- Inference: remote. The policy ran in `lerobot-policy-server` on an HF Jobs A10G (24 GB, float32, same precision as training), the laptop drove the arm with `robot.ps1 eval -Remote` through an SSH tunnel. About 0.45 s per 50-step chunk on the server, 0 errors, the arm never waited for a chunk ([docs/07](../docs/07_pi05.md))
- Protocol: [docs/08_evaluation.md](../docs/08_evaluation.md): night, desk lamp only, curtains closed; 5 spots × 8 rounds, at most 30 s per trial, all 40 in one go
- Scene: camera position and lighting matched the earlier policies' runs (shift 0–3 px). One difference: a blue-and-white object (looks like a bag) sat on the floor at the bottom edge of the overhead view, off the table, in all 40 trials; it was not there for ACT, Diffusion Policy or SmolVLA
- Recording: local dataset `Yufeng1216/rollout_eval_pi05` (under `data/`, 40 episodes)
- Result: ✅ success / 🟡 partial (grasped, not placed) / ❌ failure (not grasped)
- "Time" = recorded frames / 30. With remote inference the control loop keeps close to 30 Hz, so a trial that runs the full 30 s shows about 29 s
